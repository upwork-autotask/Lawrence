5. Performance · Development · Succession

8. Module-by-module table inventory

Core master data

central Employees Employment details — 108 columns; the hub of the entire system. Holds personal info, banking, employment terms, doc-checklist booleans (Criminalcheck, BankConfirmation, Contract, JobDescription, etc.).
lookups TbRegion · TblDeparment · TblDepot · tblSites · TblJobTitle · tblOccLevel · tblGrading · tblTaxStatus · tblTiers

Authentication

LoginDetails (UserName · Password · Admin/Editor/User flags · Level) drives tblPermissions (per-screen Admin/UserLevel1-3 flags).

Recruitment / onboarding

tblRequest → Recruitment → tblInterview/tblQue → tblEmpInterview → tblMainSHeetInterview → tblEvaluation → tblActualRecruitment → tblEmpTake → Employees Employment details. Targets & analytics: tblRecruitmentTarget · tblRecruitmentAppData · tblNonRecruitmentReason · qryRecCompare · frmRecTarget. Staging: tblTmpRec · tblTmpEmp.

Job description

TblJobTitle → TblJobdescriptionDetailRecord with children TblJobDescriptionEntryRecord · TblJobDesRolesAndResponsibility · TbJobDescriptionlKPIEntry · TblJobDesInternalTrainingEntry · TblJobDesExternalTrainingEntry · TblEmployeJobDescriptionDetails · tblEvaluation.

Training

Catalogue TblAllTrainings; bookings TblEmployeeInternalTrainingDetails · TblEmployeeExternalTrainingDetails · TblAnalysisSkils · ComboTraining; testing tblQue · tblAnswers · tblEmpTest; reporting qryEmpMarks · qryEmpResult · qryFinalResult · qryQuePerTraining · qryQueWisePts · qryTotMarks · qryTrainingWise · QryExportExcelTrainings · QryNewRecur · qryForTrainingReg.

Performance · Development · Succession

Performance: TblEmployeePerformence · TblKPI · TblKPICategory.
Development plans (with LineManager/HR/Compliance/EXCO approvals): tblDevelopement · tblQualDev · tblSkillsDev · tblDevExp.
Critical-skill / succession: tblCritical · tblCriticalSkills · tblSuccession · tblSuccAppData · TblScheme · TblScommitment · TblSPosition.

Leave

TblLeaveForm (Manager + HR approval, EmailStatus) using TblTypeofLeave.

Expenses & car scheme

TblExpense joining TblExpenseCategory · TblCostOfSale · tblActivities · tblOverheads · TblDepot · TbRegion · TblDeparment. Approvals tracked in tblApproval · qryClaimRef · qryForExp. Mileage in tblCarScheme.

Disciplinary & exit

TblDisciplinary (uses tblNatureOfOffence · tblDisciplinaryAction) plus TblCriminalReport. Exit interviews in tblExit.

9. Notes & observations

  • Hub-and-spoke design. Almost every transactional table points at EmployeeID in Employees Employment details. Renaming or splitting that table would ripple through the entire system.
  • Approval chains are baked into rows, not a separate table. Most workflows (development, leave, expenses, recruitment) store LineManager / HR / Compliance / EXCO / Approval as columns on the same record. There is one shared tblApproval for expense claims.
  • South-African HR specifics. EE (Employment Equity) groups, NBC councils, Paterson grades, and SARS tax fields are all first-class columns — this is built for SA labour-law reporting.
  • Staging tables. tblTmpRec · tblTmpEmp · tblTmpQue · tblTmpExp appear to be import buffers used by Excel-paste or migration routines (Paste Errors table confirms this).
  • Only 32 explicit FKs. Many logical joins (e.g. Employees Employment details.Department → TblDeparment.DepID) are by convention only — the database does not enforce referential integrity on them. Reports rely on Access queries to bridge those gaps.
